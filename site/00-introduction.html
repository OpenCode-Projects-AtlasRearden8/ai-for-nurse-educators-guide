Chapter 0 — Introduction: Who This Manual Is For

Education only. Never clinical advice. Everything in this manual is about building teaching materials — lesson plans, fictional cases, simulation setups, and rubrics. It is not medical guidance, not a drug reference, and not a substitute for your institution's curriculum committee, your state board, or your simulation lab's accrediting standards.

Why this manual exists

If you teach nursing students in a classroom, skills lab, or clinical rotation, you already know the math: more learners, more content, same hours. AI writing tools can genuinely help with the paper side of teaching — the dreaded lesson plan, the case study you keep meaning to rewrite, the rubric you rebuild every semester.

This manual teaches you how to use AI tools like you would use a very eager, sometimes-wrong teaching assistant:

  • Fast at producing first drafts.
  • Helpful for reorganizing, reformatting, and re-wording.
  • Unreliable on specifics — it can invent facts, guidelines, and even entire journal articles.

You stay the content expert. The AI is the fast typist.

Who it's for

  • Clinical instructors and preceptors who suddenly need teaching materials.
  • Nurse educators drafting cases, sims, and rubrics on a deadline.
  • Simulation coordinators who want faster, cleaner scenario paperwork.
  • Anyone who has never used an AI tool and does not want to feel dumb asking basic questions. There are no dumb questions here.

What you will be able to do after reading

  1. Write AI prompts that reliably produce usable lesson plan drafts.
  2. Generate fictional teaching cases that contain zero patient data.
  3. Draft simulation scenarios with objectives, setup, and debrief frames.
  4. Build rubrics that treat learners fairly and map to objectives.
  5. Run citation and hallucination checks before anything reaches a student.
  6. Keep your materials accessible — screen-reader friendly, printable, grayscale-safe, plain language.

A note on tone and tools

We deliberately avoid step-by-step click paths for specific apps ("click the blue button in the corner"). Those break every time a tool updates. Instead, each chapter teaches you the pattern — what to ask for, what to check, what to fix — in a form that works with almost any AI chat tool, including ones you might run locally on your own laptop.

Copy, adapt, and make it yours. That's the whole point.
