Level-writing rules (check before finalizing)

  • ☐ No level judges the person ("excellent student") — only the work
  • ☐ No level is impossible-in-practice
  • ☐ Each level has one concrete example fragment of work
  • ☐ Timing is its own criterion, never smuggled into a quality one
  • ☐ Examples are everyday, no idioms or culture-loaded references
  • ☐ Every criterion observable from the submitted artifact alone
